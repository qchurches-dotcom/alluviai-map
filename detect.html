UNTESTED model · tap i for what this shows
Score = shallow-gold prior × walk-from-car factork. The prior is high near a mapped anticline axis (halves about every 70 m) and where that axis runs high (reef top nearer the surface). It does not model wash downhill: that was tested twice and added nothing over distance to the line. The walk factor is high where the ground is a longer walk from a drivable track (less likely hunted out). k is the slider: 0 = prior only, 1 = equal weight, 2 = strongly favour remote ground. Shading shows only the top share of ground: Sensitive 10 %, Balanced 3 %, Strict 1 %. Lines: solid = mapped reef / anticline axes (Herman 1923, Bendigo only); dashed = reef lines inferred from old workings: groups of GeoVic shafts held to the local rock strike. Past the last shaft the reef is assumed to carry on along strike with fading confidence (halves about every 200 m, out to 1 km), and ground that rises above the level the diggers worked is down-weighted (reef top likely deeper there); ground at or below that level, e.g. a gully crossing the line, keeps full weight. Inferred lines are a guide, not a map: in central Bendigo they sit near a mapped line about twice as often as chance. Away from Bendigo every line is inferred. Taradale / Drummond / Malmsbury use 5 m interpolated terrain (1 m LiDAR there is licensed only). Neither factor has been tested against gold yet. Grey = built-up (flagged, not removed). Blank = no LiDAR or no recorded workings. Break o'Day carries the Paris line and Break o'Day anticline from Caldwell's 1929 report; the Whipstick uses 5 m terrain and lines from GeoVic reef mine sites. Land status is NOT checked: confirm access yourself.
