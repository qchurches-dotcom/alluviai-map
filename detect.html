UNTESTED model · tap i for what this shows
Score = shallow-gold prior × walk-from-car factork. The prior is high near a mapped anticline axis (halves about every 70 m) and where that axis runs high (reef top nearer the surface). It does not model wash downhill: that was tested twice and added nothing over distance to the line. The walk factor is high where the ground is a longer walk from a drivable track (less likely hunted out). k is the slider: 0 = prior only, 1 = equal weight, 2 = strongly favour remote ground. Shading shows only the top share of ground: Sensitive 10 %, Balanced 3 %, Strict 1 %. Lines: solid = mapped reef / anticline axes (Herman 1923, Bendigo only); dashed = reef lines inferred from old workings: groups of GeoVic shafts held to the local rock strike and extended 250 m past the last shaft each end (the extensions are ground the diggers may not have reached). Inferred lines are a guide, not a map: in central Bendigo they sit near a mapped line about twice as often as chance. Away from Bendigo every line is inferred. Taradale / Drummond / Malmsbury use 5 m interpolated terrain (1 m LiDAR there is licensed only). Neither factor has been tested against gold yet. Grey = built-up (flagged, not removed). Blank = no LiDAR or no mapped axis yet (the west: Kangaroo Flat, Sparrowhawk, Break o'Day). Land status is NOT checked: confirm access yourself.
